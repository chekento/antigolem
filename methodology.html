Methodology & limits

Full-text parsing. Every detected sentence is included in the statistical base. AntiGolem does not intentionally sample only a few illustrative excerpts.

D-factor. D-0, D-1 and D-2+ are heuristic labels for local reference binding and reference distance. D-2+ indicates higher ambiguity risk, not proof that a reader will misunderstand the sentence.

Framing signals. Negative-normalization, constructive framing, absolutism, modal pressure, blame cues, responsibility diffusion and over-inclusive “we” are measured using transparent lexical and structural heuristics.

Local AI cross-check. When available, a local language model may challenge or enrich the deterministic findings. Its output is treated as a second opinion rather than ground truth.

Not a diagnosis. “Golem effect”, “Pygmalion effect”, “mantra”, “ghost-context” and “psychological programming” are editorial/rhetorical labels in AntiGolem. They are not clinical findings or proof of subconscious causation.

Interpretation. Scores and danger levels are prompts for close reading. Context, audience, genre and factual content still matter.
